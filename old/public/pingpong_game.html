How to Play

A ball bounces between the paddles. Prevent it from escaping your side.

  • Host: Use W/S keys on desktop or the control panel on mobile.
  • Guest: Use Arrow keys on desktop or the control panel on mobile.
  • Score: A point is scored when the ball escapes an opponent’s side.
